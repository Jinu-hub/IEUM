# Refund & Cancellation Policy
_Last Updated: {{refund_last_updated}}_

This Refund & Cancellation Policy applies to **NexLetter**'s paid subscription services.

---

## 1. Subscription Cancellation Guide

- Users can cancel their subscription at any time from the **[Account Management] page** in their account.
- When canceling a subscription, **you can continue using the service until the end of the current paid period**, and
    
    **automatic payments will be stopped from the next billing date**.
    
- Cancellation takes effect immediately, and service access will remain until the end of the already paid period.

---

## 2. Monthly Subscription (Monthly Payment) Refund Policy

- Monthly subscription products are **digital services (SaaS) that are provided immediately upon payment completion**,
    
    **refunds are not available after service usage has begun**.
    
- However, **full refunds are exceptionally available** only when **all of the following conditions are met**.

### ✅ Exceptional Refund Conditions

- Within **7 days from the payment date**
- **No usage history of paid features** such as newsletter creation, sending, or downloading

If the above conditions are not met, monthly subscription products are not eligible for refunds, and
    
**only automatic payments from the next billing cycle will be stopped** upon cancellation.

---

## 3. Annual Subscription (Annual Payment) Refund Policy

- Annual subscription products are **prepaid products with discounted pricing based on 12 months of usage**.
- In case of mid-term cancellation, **the discount benefit is not applied**, and
    
    **a refund is provided after deducting the amount based on the monthly regular price for the used period**.
    

### 🔹 Refund Amount Calculation Method

```
Refund Amount = Annual Payment Amount − (Monthly Regular Price × Number of Months Used)
```

- The number of months used is calculated in **1-month increments from the payment date**, and
    
    **any period less than 1 month is considered as 1 month**.
    
- If the amount already used equals or exceeds the annual payment amount, the refund amount will be ₩0.

### 🔹 Example

- Monthly Regular Price: **$5**
- Annual Payment Amount: **$48** (20% discount applied)
- Cancellation after 4 months of use

```
→ $5 × 4 months = $20 deducted
→ Refund Amount: $28
```

---

## 4. Refund Processing Method and Processing Time

- If you are eligible for a refund upon subscription cancellation, **the refund amount will be automatically calculated and processed by the system**.
- When automatic refund is applicable, **payment cancellation or refund will be processed within 3-5 business days**.
- The actual refund reflection time may vary depending on the card company's policy.
- If you have any objections regarding the refund amount calculation or processing results,
please contact us at {{support email}} or through the inquiry page.

---

## 5. Cases Where Refunds Are Restricted

Refunds may be restricted in the following cases:

- When service usage history is confirmed
- Payments that include free benefits or promotions
- When terms violations or fraudulent use is confirmed
- When the refund amount is ₩0
